## Hello & Servus 👋

I'm Susanne, a PhD student at [Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)](https://www.tue.nl/en/). I work at the intersection of **cyber threat intelligence** and **machine learning**: turning messy, informal text from underground communities into structured knowledge.

- 🔭 Currently working on information extraction (entities and relations) and knowledge graphs for cyber threat intelligence, with compact models trained on LLM-generated labels
- 🧠 Interested in AI for security, learning from noisy and LLM-generated labels, evaluation under ambiguity (annotation quality, span boundaries, matching rules), efficient models, entity resolution, and neurosymbolic methods
